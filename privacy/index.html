Spark Assistant: privacy policy

Spark Assistant is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

It accesses that account's data only to do what the owner asks. The data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone, other than the services needed to deliver results to the owner.

Spark Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
